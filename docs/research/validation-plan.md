# Motexa — Validation Plan

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this validation plan is to determine whether Motexa solves a real problem for vehicle owners and automotive service providers.

The MVP should validate the core product before significant investment is made into the larger Motexa ecosystem.

---

# 2. Core Hypothesis

> Vehicle owners need a trusted and intelligent way to understand vehicle problems and find the appropriate solution.

Motexa will test whether AutoAI can become the first step in that process.

---

# 3. What We Need to Validate

The initial validation focuses on six questions:

1. Do vehicle owners experience the problem?
2. Do they want help understanding vehicle problems?
3. Is AutoAI useful?
4. Do users trust Motexa's recommendations?
5. Will users connect with automotive specialists through Motexa?
6. Will providers participate?
