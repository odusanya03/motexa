# Motexa — Market Assumptions

## 1. Purpose

This document records the assumptions currently being made about Motexa's target market, users, providers, technology, and business model.

These are **hypotheses, not confirmed facts**.

They should be validated through user interviews, provider interviews, surveys, usage data, and MVP experiments as resources become available.

---

# 2. Market Assumption

There is a significant need for easier access to reliable automotive information and services.

Vehicle owners frequently need help with:

- Diagnosing problems
- Finding mechanics
- Finding genuine parts
- Understanding repair costs
- Finding towing services
- Inspecting used vehicles
- Maintaining their vehicles

---

# 3. Customer Problem Assumption

Vehicle owners often experience uncertainty when something goes wrong with their vehicle.

They may not know:

```text
What is wrong?
        ↓
How serious is it?
        ↓
Can I drive the vehicle?
        ↓
Can I fix it myself?
        ↓
Who should I contact?
