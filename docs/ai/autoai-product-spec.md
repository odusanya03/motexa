# Motexa — AutoAI Product Specification

## 1. Purpose

AutoAI is Motexa's AI-powered automotive assistant.

Its purpose is to help vehicle owners understand vehicle problems, identify possible causes, find relevant information, learn how to perform appropriate DIY checks, and connect with verified automotive professionals when professional assistance is required.

AutoAI is an assistance and decision-support system, not a replacement for a qualified automotive technician.
