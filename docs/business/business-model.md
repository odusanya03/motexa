# Motexa — Business Model

## 1. Overview

Motexa is an automotive technology platform that connects vehicle owners with trusted automotive services, information, tools, parts, and intelligent assistance.

The platform combines:

- AI-powered automotive assistance
- Verified automotive specialists
- Vehicle diagnostics
- DIY resources
- Vehicle inspection
- Genuine parts discovery
- Towing services
- Vehicle rental and transportation
- Automotive tools and equipment

The MVP will focus on validating the core user experience before expanding into a full automotive marketplace.

---

# 2. Core Value Proposition

Motexa helps vehicle owners answer:

> "What is wrong with my vehicle, what should I do next, and who can help me?"

Instead of searching across multiple disconnected platforms, users can access automotive assistance from one place.

---

# 3. Customer Segments

## Primary

### Private Vehicle Owners

People who own cars and need:

- Maintenance
- Repairs
- Diagnostics
- Parts
- Mechanics
- Inspections
- Emergency assistance

### Drivers

People who operate vehicles but may not own them.

### DIY Enthusiasts

Users who want to:

- Understand vehicle problems
- Learn repairs
- Find tutorials
- Purchase tools
- Perform basic maintenance

## Automotive Businesses

- Parts dealers

---

# 5. Initial Geographic Focus

The MVP should initially target Nigeria.

The platform can later expand to other African markets and eventually international markets.

The initial focus allows Motexa to understand:

- Local automotive problems
- User behavior
- Pricing
- Trust issues
- Payment preferences
- Verification requirements
- Service-provider behavior
