# Motexa — Monetization Strategy

## 1. Objective

Motexa should first establish user trust, marketplace activity, and product usefulness before aggressively optimizing revenue.

The monetization strategy should allow the platform to generate revenue from multiple complementary services without compromising the user experience.

---

# 2. Monetization Principles

Motexa monetization should be:

- Transparent
- Fair
- Scalable
- Relevant to the service provided
- Sustainable
- Aligned with user value

Motexa should never recommend a service simply because it generates a higher commission.

---

# 3. Primary Revenue Streams

Potential revenue sources include:

1. Service commissions
2. Provider subscriptions
3. Parts marketplace commissions
4. Automotive equipment sales
5. Inspection fees
6. Towing commissions
7. Vehicle rental commissions
8. AutoAI premium subscriptions
9. Advertising and promoted listings
10. Fleet and enterprise subscriptions
11. Partnerships and referrals

---

# 4. Service Commission

Motexa may charge a percentage of completed service transactions.

Service value:       ₦50,000
