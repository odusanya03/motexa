# Motexa — Provider Model

## 1. Purpose

Motexa connects vehicle owners with trusted automotive service providers.

This document defines how providers are onboarded, verified, represented, ranked, and connected with customers.

---

# 2. Provider Categories

Motexa may support:

- General mechanics
- Auto electricians
- Diagnostic specialists
- Mercedes specialists
- Toyota specialists
- Transmission specialists
- Engine specialists
- AC specialists
- Brake specialists
- Suspension specialists
- Tire specialists
- Body repair specialists
- Auto parts suppliers
- Vehicle inspectors
- Tow operators
- Rental providers
