# Motexa — User Personas

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary users of Motexa, their goals, frustrations, behaviours, motivations, technical knowledge, and expectations.

Because Motexa is currently in the MVP/prototype stage and broad customer/provider research has not yet been conducted, these personas are working models.

Each persona should therefore be treated as a hypothesis that will be refined through real-world interviews, usability testing, and product analytics.

---

# 2. Persona Classification

Motexa has four primary personas:

| Persona | Role | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle Owner | Customer / Vehicle User | Primary |
| Automotive Specialist | Service Provider | Primary |
| Mobile Specialist | Field Service Provider | Secondary |
| Motexa Operations Admin | Platform Operator | Essential |

The first version of Motexa should prioritize the **Vehicle Owner** experience while ensuring the **Automotive Specialist** experience is strong enough to support the marketplace.

---

# 3. Persona 1 — Vehicle Owner

## 3.1 Overview

The Vehicle Owner is the primary Motexa user.

They own or regularly use one or more vehicles and need help understanding, maintaining, diagnosing, repairing, or managing them.

They may have little or no automotive knowledge.

---

## 3.2 Example Profile

**Name:** Daniel

**Age:** 25–45

**Occupation:** Professional / Business owner / Employee

**Location:** Urban or semi-urban area

**Vehicle:** Used or relatively new personal vehicle

**Automotive knowledge:** Beginner to intermediate

**Technology comfort:** Comfortable with smartphones and common apps

**Motexa role:** Customer

**Persona status:** Working hypothesis

---

## 3.3 Goals

The Vehicle Owner wants to:

- Keep their vehicle reliable.
- Understand what is wrong when problems occur.
- Avoid unnecessary repairs.
- Find trustworthy mechanics.
- Know whether a problem is urgent.
- Get help quickly during emergencies.
- Understand repair estimates.
- Keep vehicle records.
- Know when maintenance is due.
- Reduce the stress associated with vehicle problems.
- Make better decisions about repairs.

---

## 3.4 Frustrations

Common frustrations may include:

- Not understanding vehicle problems.
- Mechanics using technical terminology they do not understand.
- Uncertainty about whether a repair is actually necessary.
- Difficulty finding trustworthy specialists.
- Fear of being overcharged.
- Poor communication from workshops.
- Losing service receipts.
- Not remembering previous repairs.
- Having to contact several people during a breakdown.
- Searching through YouTube or Google without knowing which information applies to their specific vehicle.

---

## 3.5 Behaviour

The Vehicle Owner may:

- Search Google when something goes wrong.
- Watch YouTube repair videos.
- Ask friends or family for recommendations.
- Ask questions on Reddit, Quora, Facebook or WhatsApp groups.
- Contact mechanics through phone calls or messaging apps.
- Visit nearby workshops.
- Use an OBD scanner if they have access to one.
- Take pictures of warning lights.
- Keep vehicle documents physically or on their phone.
- Rely heavily on trusted mechanics.

These behaviours represent potential integration or transition points for Motexa.

---

## 3.6 Automotive Knowledge

The Vehicle Owner can fall anywhere on a broad spectrum.

### Beginner

May know:

- Basic dashboard warning lights.
- How to fuel the vehicle.
- How to check basic fluids.
- When servicing is generally required.

May not know:

- Engine components.
- Diagnostic codes.
- Electrical systems.
- Mechanical terminology.
- How to distinguish symptoms from causes.

### Intermediate

May understand:

- Basic maintenance.
- Common vehicle problems.
- Basic OBD codes.
- Common components.
- Basic troubleshooting.

### Advanced

May be able to:

- Use diagnostic equipment.
- Interpret basic fault codes.
- Perform maintenance.
- Carry out some repairs.

Motexa should support all three groups without assuming automotive expertise.

---

## 3.7 Trust Concerns

The Vehicle Owner needs confidence that:

- The specialist is a real person.
- The specialist is legitimate.
- The specialist has relevant experience.
- Reviews are genuine.
- The specialist actually performs the advertised service.
- The quotation is transparent.
- Motexa will not expose them to unnecessary risk.

Trust is particularly important for expensive repairs.

---

## 3.8 AutoAI Expectations

The Vehicle Owner does not necessarily want a technically sophisticated AI.

They want understandable answers.

Instead of:

> "Possible fuel-trim deviation caused by an unmetered-air condition."

they may prefer:

> "Your engine may be receiving more air than the system expects. A common cause is an air/vacuum leak, although other issues can produce the same code."

The AI should translate technical information into understandable language.
