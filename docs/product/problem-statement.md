# Motexa — Problem Statement

## 1. Executive Summary

Vehicle ownership involves fragmented, trust-sensitive, and often technical problems.

A vehicle owner may know that something is wrong with their vehicle without understanding the cause. Finding a competent and trustworthy specialist can be difficult, repair estimates can be unclear, and maintenance records are often scattered across paper receipts, WhatsApp conversations, workshop notebooks, and memory.

Motexa aims to connect these problems through a vehicle-centered platform.

The initial workflow is:

**Vehicle Context → AI-Assisted Diagnosis → Specialist Discovery → Service Request → Quote → Booking → Repair → Service History**

The broader Motexa ecosystem can eventually expand into:

- Parts
- Towing
- Roadside assistance
- Vehicle inspection
- Vehicle history
- Car rental
- Transportation
- Insurance integrations
- Fleet management
- Diagnostic equipment
- Predictive maintenance

---

# 2. Core Problems

## 2.1 Vehicle Owners Often Do Not Understand What Is Wrong

Most private vehicle owners are not automotive technicians.

A warning light, unusual noise, vibration, overheating, starting problem, fluid leak, or change in vehicle behaviour can create uncertainty.

The owner may ask:

- What is wrong with my car?
- Is it safe to drive?
- How serious is the problem?
- Can I fix it myself?
- What kind of mechanic or specialist do I need?
- How much might it cost?
- Do I need to take the car to a workshop immediately?

Today, users often have to research these questions independently.

---

# 3. Finding a Trustworthy Specialist

Vehicle owners often find mechanics through:

- Friends and family
- Word of mouth
- WhatsApp
- Social media
- Google/search engines
- Previous personal experience
- Local workshops
- Dealerships

These methods can work, but they do not consistently provide structured information about:

- Identity
- Business legitimacy
- Specialization
- Experience
- Location
- Availability
- Customer ratings
- Completed jobs
- Reputation
- Service history

Motexa aims to make automotive specialist discovery more structured and transparent.

---

# 4. Automotive Expertise Is Fragmented

A vehicle problem may require a specific type of specialist.

Examples include:

- General mechanic
- Diagnostic technician
- Auto electrician
- AC specialist
- Transmission specialist
- Engine specialist
- Suspension specialist
- Brake specialist
- Body repair specialist
- Tyre specialist
- Mercedes specialist
- Toyota specialist
- BMW specialist
- Other brand/model specialists

A vehicle owner who does not understand the technical problem may not know which specialist to contact.

Motexa should help translate the user's problem into an appropriate service category.

---

# 5. Repair Pricing Can Be Difficult to Understand

Vehicle owners may receive vague statements such as:

> "Your car needs a new sensor."

or:

> "The engine needs some work."

The customer may not know:

- Which component is faulty
- Why it needs replacement
- Whether it was actually tested
- Whether the part is genuine
- Whether an OEM or aftermarket alternative exists
- How much the part costs
- How much labour costs
- Whether the repair is urgent
- Whether another specialist would recommend the same repair

Motexa will not attempt to guarantee that every quote is fair.

Instead, the MVP should provide structured quotations that make it easier to understand and compare available service options.

---

# 6. Vehicle Service History Is Fragmented

Vehicle information can be scattered across:

- Paper receipts
- WhatsApp messages
- Workshop records
- Invoices
- Bank/payment records
- Phone galleries
- Individual mechanics
- The owner's memory

This makes it difficult for an owner to answer:

> "What has actually been done to this vehicle?"

Motexa should create a persistent digital service history that can eventually follow the vehicle throughout its ownership lifecycle.

---

# 7. Vehicle Breakdowns Create Multiple Problems

A breakdown can create several simultaneous needs:

- Diagnosis
- Mechanic
- Towing
- Roadside assistance
- Transportation
- Parts
- Temporary replacement vehicle
- Communication with another party

Car breaks down
      ↓
Need to know what happened
      ↓
Need a mechanic

      ↓
Need alternative transportation
